Chapter 13

When the Bell Rings

Events and Event Handlers

🔔

A little bell hung on the tailor's door. The bell did not ring by itself. It rang when someone opened the door. And when it rang, the tailor looked up and said, "Welcome!"

"The ring is an event," said the tailor. "It is something that happens. What I do next is the event handler. It is my rule: when this happens, do that."

Programs have little bells too. A mouse click. A key press. A finger tap. A timer ring. The program waits. Then it runs the handler.

Waiting for something to happen is called being event-driven.

Big Idea

An event is something that happens. An event handler is what you do when it happens.

Try It!

Name three events in a game you like. What happens when each one occurs? For example: when I touch a coin, I get a point.
