Chapter 21

The Chin-Chin Cutter Family

Object-Oriented Basics

🥟

In the kitchen drawer lived a star-shaped chin-chin cutter. It was not a chin-chin. It was the idea of a chin-chin. That is a class. Press it into dough and you get a real star chin-chin. That is an object.

Each chin-chin was different. One was golden. One was brown. One had a missing corner. Those are its properties.

Each chin-chin could do things. It could be decorated. It could be eaten. Things an object can do are its methods.

One day a new cutter came. It was a star with a little cap on top. It was made from the star cutter and just added a cap. "That is inheritance," said the big cutter. "It got everything from me, and then a cap."

One cutter, many chin-chin. Same recipe, each one its own little self.

Big Idea

A class is a plan. An object is one thing made from the plan. Objects have properties (what they are) and methods (what they do). Inheritance lets a new class build on an old one.

Try It!

Make a class called Pet. What properties does it have? Name, colour, legs? What methods? Speak, eat, sleep? Now make two pets.
