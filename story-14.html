Chapter 14

The Train of Puff-Puffs

Collections — Lists and Arrays

🚂

Baker Bisi made a train from cake boxes. Each box held one puff-puff. Each box had a number. Box 0 held plain. Box 1 held coconut. Box 2 held ginger.

"Why does the first box have 0?" asked her helper. "Because computers count from zero!" said Bisi. "The number is the index. What is inside is the element."

She counted: three boxes. "That is the length." When a new puff-puff came, she added a box. When one was eaten, the boxes moved up.

A train of things in order is called a list or an array.

Big Idea

A list holds many things in order. Each thing is an element. Its place is the index. The number of things is the length.

Try It!

Make a list of five friends. Who is at 0? Who is at 4? How many friends in all?
