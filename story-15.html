Chapter 15

The Alphabet Soup

Strings and Text Processing

🍜

In the soup lived a string named "jambo." It was not a word. It was beads on a line: j, a, m, b, o.

The string could tell you its length: five beads. It could show you bead 1: "a." It could shout "JAMBO!" or whisper "jambo."

One day "nyama" met "choma." They hugged and became "nyamachoma." That is joining.

"Text is just letters in a row," said the spoon. "Every program that talks to people moves letters around."

Big Idea

A string is text. It is letters in a row. You can count it, find a letter, make it loud, and join strings together.

Try It!

Write your name. Count the letters. Now write it in CAPITALS. Now write it backwards, one letter at a time.
