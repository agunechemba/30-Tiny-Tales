Chapter 3

Speaking Computer

Code, Statement, Syntax

🐢

A little tortoise lived on a hill. He only spoke computer. "Hello," said Ada. The tortoise said nothing.

So Ada learned his words. One line is one idea. She said "hide()" and the tortoise hid in his shell. She said "walk(3)" and he walked three steps. Each line is a statement.

But the tortoise was fussy. If Ada forgot a bracket, he sneezed. If she spelled a word wrong, he hiccuped. The fussy rules are called syntax.

"Why so fussy?" asked Ada. "Because," said the tortoise, "if I guess, I might do the wrong thing!"

Big Idea

Code is made of lines. Each line tells the computer one thing to do. The rules for writing it are called syntax.

Try It!

Say this sentence: "the cat sat the mat on." It sounds funny! Now fix it: "the cat sat on the mat." You just fixed syntax!
