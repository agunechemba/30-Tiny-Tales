Chapter 2

The Robot's Breakfast

Program, Instruction, Sequence

🤖

Tino the robot wanted toast. “Easy!” said Sam. “Toast the bread.” Tino nodded, picked up the whole loaf, and put it in the toaster. Bread everywhere.

“No, no,” laughed Sam. “You need instructions — small, clear steps. One at a time.” So Sam wrote them down:

1. Take one slice of bread. 2. Put the slice in the toaster. 3. Press the lever down. 4. Wait. 5. Take the toast out. 6. Spread butter.

Tino followed every line perfectly. But when Sam tried to help by moving step 6 to the top, Tino buttered a cold, hard slice of bread and looked very confused.

“Ah,” said Sam. “The order matters too.” A list of instructions in the right order is called a program, and the order itself is called sequence.

Big Idea

A program is a list of instructions. Computers do them exactly as written, one after another — so the order (the sequence) matters enormously.

Try It!

Write the instructions for brushing your teeth, one step per line. Now swap two lines and read it out loud. What silly thing happens?
