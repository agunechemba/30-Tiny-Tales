Chapter 19

The Lost Cowrie

Algorithms — Searching, Sorting, Efficiency

🔍

A cowrie hid in one of one hundred calabashes. "Guess," said Grandpa. "Number 1? No. Number 2? No." That could take all day!

"Let me try," said Kwame. "Is it in 1 to 50?" No. "51 to 75?" No. "76 to 88?" Yes! Each question cut the work in half. Kwame found it in seven guesses. That is binary search.

Then Grandpa spilled numbered cards. Kwame picked them up and put each one in its place. "That is sorting," said Grandpa. "Putting things in order."

"An algorithm is a plan," said Kwame. "And efficiency is using few steps." "Yes," said Grandpa. "Fewer steps means more time for chin-chin!"

Big Idea

An algorithm is a step-by-step plan. Searching finds one thing. Sorting puts things in order. Few steps is better than many steps.

Try It!

Play guess-the-number from 1 to 100. Cut the numbers in half each time. Can you win in seven guesses?
