Chapter 16

Inside the Treehouse

Scope and Lifetime

🌳

Kofi built a treehouse. It had a secret password, a mat, and a tin of groundnuts. Everything inside was only for the treehouse. The password only worked inside. Shout it outside and nothing happened.

"Those are local things," said Dad. "They live in one place." He pointed at the sun. "The sun is global. Everyone can see it."

One more rule: when the treehouse was packed away, the groundnuts went with it. Local things go away when their place goes away. That is their lifetime.

"So a variable inside a function," said Kofi, "is a groundnut in a treehouse." "Exactly," said Dad.

Big Idea

Local things live in one place. Global things are everywhere. Lifetime is how long a thing lasts.

Try It!

Local or global? Your name inside a "say hello" function? The sun? A loop counter? The year?
