Chapter 16

Inside the Treehouse

Scope and Lifetime

🌳

Kai built a treehouse with a secret password, a beanbag and a biscuit tin. Everything inside belonged to the treehouse. The password only worked inside — shout it from the garden and nothing happened.

“Those are local things,” said Kai’s dad. “They live inside one place and only that place can use them.” He pointed at the sun. “The sun is global. Everyone can see it, from the garden, from the treehouse, from the road.”

There was one more rule: when Kai climbed down and the treehouse was packed away, the biscuit tin went with it. Local things disappear when their place disappears. That’s their lifetime.

“So a variable made inside a function,” said Kai slowly, “is a biscuit tin in a treehouse.” “Exactly,” said Dad. “And if you leave the lid off a global one, everybody knows about it.”

Big Idea

Scope is where a variable can be seen. Local variables live inside one function; global ones are visible everywhere. Lifetime is how long they last.

Try It!

Local or global? Your name (in a “say hello” function) · the app’s high score · a loop counter · the current year.
