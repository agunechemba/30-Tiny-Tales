Chapter 6

Boxes with Names

Variables and Assignment

📦

Grandpa had many boxes. Each box had a name on it. One said "millet." One said "groundnuts." One said "snacks."

"A box with a name is a variable," said Grandpa. "The name is the label. What is inside is the value."

He put three mangoes in the box called "snacks." Then he took them out and put in a banana. "Where did the mangoes go?" asked Ada. "A box holds one thing," said Grandpa. "Put a new thing in, and the old thing is gone."

Ada made a box called "score." She put 0 in it. Then she won a game and put 10 in. Same box. New number!

Big Idea

A variable is a box with a name. It holds one thing at a time. When you put in something new, the old thing goes away.

Try It!

Draw three boxes. Name them "name," "age," and "score." Fill them in. Now change "score" to a bigger number. What happened to the old number?
