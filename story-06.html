Chapter 6

Boxes with Names

Variables and Assignment

📦

In Grandpa’s workshop there were hundreds of wooden boxes, and every box had a label. “Apples,” said one. “Screws,” said another. “Grandma’s birthday,” said a third (that one held cake).

“A box with a label is a variable,” said Grandpa. “The label is its name. What’s inside is its value.” He put three apples in the box marked snacks. Then he took them out and put in a banana instead.

“Wait — where did the apples go?” asked Ada. “A box holds one thing at a time,” said Grandpa. “Put something new in, and the old thing is gone. That’s assignment — you tell the box what it holds now.”

Ada wrote a label on a fresh box: score. She put a 0 inside. Then, after winning a game, she replaced it with a 10. Same box. Same name. New value.

Big Idea

A variable is a named box that holds a value. Assigning a new value replaces the old one — the name stays, the contents change.

Try It!

Draw three boxes labelled name, age and score. Fill them in. Now change score to something bigger. What happened to the old number?
