Chapter 9

If It Rains

Conditionals and Branching

🌦️

Every morning Zainab looked at the sky. If it rained, she took an umbrella. If it was sunny, she took sunglasses. Then she went to school.

"You are using a conditional," said Mum. "Your day splits like a road. If it rains, go this way. Else, go that way."

Zainab saw forks everywhere. At the traffic light: if green, walk. Else, wait. In her game: if score is 100, you win. Else, keep playing.

Sometimes there are three roads: if rain, umbrella. Else if cold, scarf. Else, sunglasses.

"The computer is not clever," said Mum. "It just checks the question and takes the right road."

Big Idea

If something is true, do this. Else, do that. This is called a conditional.

Try It!

Make an if/else for a traffic light. If green, what? If red, what? What about yellow?
