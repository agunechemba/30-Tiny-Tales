Chapter 10

The Hundred Tiles

Loops and Iteration

🔁

The kitchen floor had one hundred tiles, and every one of them was muddy. Sam began scrubbing: tile one, tile two, tile three… By tile eleven, Sam was bored and by tile twenty, very bored indeed.

Then Grandma showed him a magic trick. “Repeat one hundred times: scrub a tile, step forward.” Sam said it once, and the floor cleaned itself, tile by tile, all the way to the end.

“That’s a loop,” said Grandma. “A for loop is when you know how many times to repeat. A while loop is when you repeat until something changes — like while the floor is still muddy, keep scrubbing.”

Sam looked at his bowl of noodles at dinner and grinned. While there is food on the plate: take a bite. He’d been looping his whole life without knowing it.

Big Idea

A loop repeats instructions instead of writing them again and again. Use for when you know the count, and while when you’re waiting for a condition to change.

Try It!

Find the loop in these: eating a bowl of noodles, walking up ten stairs, singing “Happy Birthday”. Which ones are for loops?
