Chapter 10

The Hundred Tiles

Loops and Iteration

🔁

The yard had one hundred stones. All were muddy. Sani washed stone one. Then stone two. Then stone three. He was very tired!

Grandma showed him a trick. "Say this: Repeat one hundred times: wash a stone and move on." Sani said it once. The stones got clean, one by one!

"That is a loop," said Grandma. "A for loop is when you know how many times. A while loop is when you repeat until something stops — like: while the yard is muddy, keep washing."

Sani looked at his food. While there is food, take a bite. He had been looping all his life!

Big Idea

A loop repeats things. Use for when you know the count. Use while when you wait for something to stop.

Try It!

Find the loop in these: eating a bowl of rice, walking up ten steps, singing "Happy Birthday." Which one is a for loop?
