Chapter 5

The Wobbly Bug

Bugs and Debugging

🐛

Chidi made a game. A little goat runs on the screen. But when he pressed play, the goat ran backwards and fell off! "It's broken!" he cried.

His sister looked. "You have a bug. A bug is one little mistake. Let's find it."

They played the game again. The goat fell off every time. Good! A bug you can see again is easy to catch.

They looked at the lines. There it was! A minus sign where a plus sign should be. They fixed it. The goat ran the right way!

"Fixing a bug is called debugging," said his sister. "Then you play again to be sure."

Big Idea

A bug is a mistake. To fix it: make it happen again, find it, fix it, and test again.
