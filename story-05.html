Chapter 5

The Wobbly Bug

Bugs and Debugging

🐛

Ollie built a game where a little cat runs across the screen. But when he pressed play, the cat ran backwards and fell off the left edge. “The game is broken!” he wailed.

His big sister peered at the screen. “You have a bug — one tiny mistake hiding in your code. Let’s be detectives.”

First they made it happen again. A bug you can repeat is a bug you can catch. Then they hunted: which line moves the cat? There it was — a minus sign where a plus sign should be. They fixed it, and played the game again. This time the cat ran the right way.

“Fixing it is called debugging,” said his sister, “and the best part is testing it afterwards to be sure.” Ollie decided bugs weren’t scary at all. They were just clues.

Big Idea

A bug is a mistake in a program. Debugging means: make it happen again, find the culprit, fix it, then test it once more.
