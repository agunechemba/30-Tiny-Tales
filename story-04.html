In and Out

Input and Output

🚪

There was a doorbell at a shop. When you pressed it, it asked, "Who are you?" You said your name. It said, "Welcome, Zuri!"

Zuri thought about it all day. Pressing the button is something going in. The welcome is something coming out.

Later, Zuri made zobo. Flowers and water go in. Cold zobo comes out. The things going in are the input. The things coming out are the output.

Every program has a door for things going in and a door for things coming out.

Big Idea

Input is what you give the program. Output is what the program gives back.

Try It!

Look at a blender. What goes in? What comes out? Now try a toy car. What goes in? What comes out?
