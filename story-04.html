In and Out

Input and Output

🚪

There was once a very polite doorbell. When you pressed it, it asked, “Who’s there?” You said your name, and it replied, “Welcome, Ruby!”

Ruby thought about this all afternoon. Pressing the button was something going in. The greeting was something coming out. The input goes into the machine; the output comes back to you.

Later, at her lemonade stand, Ruby saw it everywhere. Lemons, sugar and water went in. Lemonade came out. She typed numbers into a calculator — out came the answer.

“Every program,” said her dad, “is a little machine with a door for things going in and a door for things coming out. Sometimes it only has one door. Sometimes it has lots.”

Big Idea

Input is what you give the program. Output is what the program gives back — on a screen, in a sound, or as a movement.

Try It!

Look at a microwave. What are its inputs? What are its outputs? Now try a video game controller.
