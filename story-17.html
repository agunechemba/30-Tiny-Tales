Chapter 17

The Borrowed Toolbox

Modules, Libraries and APIs

🧰

Nneka wanted to build a stool. First she made a hammer from a stone and a stick. It took all morning. It was a bad hammer.

"Why not use the toolbox?" asked her neighbour. Inside were hammers and saws that other people made. "A library is a toolbox of code. You borrow a tool and get on with your job."

Later Nneka needed wood. She did not cut a tree. She went to the timber yard and asked for a plank. "That is an API," said her neighbour. "A friendly counter. You ask the right way, you get an answer."

Nneka finished the stool before lunch!

Big Idea

A library is code someone else made. You can use it. An API is the way you ask for something and get it back.
