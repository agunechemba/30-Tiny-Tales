Chapter 12

Femi the Function

Functions, Parameters and Return Values

⚙️

Femi was a small machine in the corner of the carpenter’s shed. On the front was a slot, and above the slot was a sign: “Give me a number. I’ll give you double.”

You put in a 4, and out popped an 8. You put in a 10, and out popped a 20. You could do it a thousand times and Femi never complained.

“The slot is a parameter,” said the carpenter. “The 4 you drop in is an argument. The 8 that comes out is the return value. And asking Femi to do his job is called calling him.”

Femi had friends, too: a machine that made chapati (you gave it the flour), a machine that said hello (you gave it a name). Each one had a name, a slot, and something to give back — so you never had to build the same machine twice.

Big Idea

A function is a reusable little machine. You give it arguments, it uses its parameters, and it hands back a return value. Call it as often as you like.

Try It!

Design a function called makeChapati(flour). What goes in the slot? What comes back out? Now design greet(name).
