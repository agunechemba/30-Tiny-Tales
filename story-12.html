Chapter 12

Femi the Function

Functions, Parameters and Return Values

⚙️

Femi was a small machine. On the front was a sign: "Give me a number. I will give you double."

You put in 4. Out came 8. You put in 10. Out came 20. You could do it all day. Femi never got tired.

"The slot is a parameter," said the carpenter. "The 4 you put in is an argument. The 8 that comes out is the return value. Asking Femi to work is calling him."

Femi had friends too. One made chapati. One said hello. Each had a name, a slot, and something to give back.

Big Idea

A function is a little machine. You give it something. It does a job. It gives something back. You can use it again and again.

Try It!

Make a function called makeChapati(flour). What goes in the slot? What comes out? Now make greet(name).
