Chapter 8

The Math Magician

Operators

✨

The Math Magician had a belt full of tiny wands. “Each wand does one trick,” she told the children, “and only one.”

She waved the arithmetic wands: + joined numbers, − took away, * multiplied, / shared out fairly. Then she drew out the comparison wands: > asked “is this bigger?”, < asked “is this smaller?”, and == asked “are these exactly the same?” They always answered with a yes or a no.

Last came the logical wands, the strangest of all. AND said yes only if both things were true. OR said yes if either was true. And NOT — cheeky NOT — turned every yes into a no and every no into a yes.

“Remember,” said the Magician, “a wand can only do its own trick. Ask the wrong wand the wrong question and you’ll get nonsense.”

Big Idea

Arithmetic operators (+ − * /) work on numbers. Comparison operators (> < ==) give true or false. Logical operators (AND, OR, NOT) combine true/false answers.

Try It!

Work out: 5 > 3 · 2 > 7 · true AND false · NOT false. Then invent one of your own.
