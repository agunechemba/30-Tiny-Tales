Chapter 8

The Math Magician

Operators

✨

The Math Magician had tiny wands. "Each wand does one trick," she said. "Only one."

She waved the adding wands. + puts numbers together. − takes away. * makes more. / shares out.

Then she showed the asking wands. > asks, "Is this bigger?" < asks, "Is this smaller?" == asks, "Are these the same?" They always say yes or no.

Last were the yes/no wands. AND says yes only if both are yes. OR says yes if one is yes. NOT turns yes into no and no into yes. Cheeky NOT!

Big Idea

Some wands do math: + − * /. Some ask questions: > < ==. Some join yes and no: AND, OR, NOT.

Try It!

Say yes or no: Is 5 bigger than 3? Is 2 bigger than 7? Is "yes AND no" true? What about "NOT no"?
